## Sibling Dynamics

- 6yo + 4yo: Competition is normal. Give each 1-on-1 time. Praise cooperation, not just individuals
- Both boys + baby sister: Involve them in "helping" with Arbel (fetching diapers, singing to her)
- Jealousy toward baby is expected — validate feelings, don't dismiss

## Daily Routine Tips

### Morning
- Prep bags/clothes night before (game changer with 3 kids)
- Avior can dress himself — build independence
- Nitai needs help but let him choose between 2 options
- Arbel: feed first, she's the wildcard

### Meals
- Family meals together when possible
- Age-appropriate foods: Arbel on soft finger foods, boys can help set table
- Don't stress about picky eating — exposure > forcing
- Meal prep on Friday for the week ahead

### Bedtime
- Stagger: Arbel first (7-7:30), then Nitai (7:30-8), then Avior (8-8:30)
- Routine = predictability = calm: bath → PJs → story → lights out
- Avior can have quiet reading time in bed
- White noise for Arbel if brothers are loud

- Avior: LEGO, drawing, simple card games, audiobooks in Hebrew

- Shabbat walks as a family

## Health Quick Reference

### When to Call the Doctor
- Fever >38.5°C in Arbel, >39°C in boys lasting >24h
- Persistent vomiting/diarrhea (dehydration risk, especially Arbel)
- Difficulty breathing, rash with fever
- Head injury with vomiting or behavior change
- Trust parental instinct — if something feels off, call

### Common Issues by Age
- **Arbel:** Teething (cold teether, Tsinun), diaper rash, sleep regression
- **Nitai:** Night terrors (don't wake, just be present), growing pains, ear infections
- **Avior:** Sports injuries, loose teeth, stomach aches (sometimes anxiety-related)

## Parenting Philosophy Reminders

- **Good enough parenting IS good parenting** — perfection isn't the goal
- **Repair matters more than prevention** — lost your cool? Apologize. Model emotional maturity
- **Play is learning** — especially for under-6
- **Connection before correction** — get on their level, empathize, then redirect
- **Rest is productive** — burned-out parents can't be present parents
