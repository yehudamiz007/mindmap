Analyze systematically:
1. **Identify asset, timeframe, and chart type**

3. **Key levels** — supply/demand zones, liquidity pools, FVGs

## Advanced Methodologies

### 1. Smart Money Concepts (ICT)

**Market Structure:**
- **BOS (Break of Structure)** — trend continuation confirmation
- **CHoCH (Change of Character)** — first sign of reversal
- **Higher TF structure > Lower TF structure** — always

**Order Blocks:**
- Last opposite candle before impulsive move
- Bullish OB: last bearish candle before bullish impulse
- Bearish OB: last bullish candle before bearish impulse
- Mitigation: price returns to OB and reacts → valid entry
- Refined OB: use lower TF to narrow the zone

**Liquidity Concepts:**
- **Buy-side liquidity (BSL):** Equal highs, swing highs, trendline highs — magnets for price
- **Sell-side liquidity (SSL):** Equal lows, swing lows, trendline lows
- **Liquidity sweep/grab:** Price takes out stops then reverses — key signal
- **Stop hunts:** Engineered moves to trigger stops before real direction

**Fair Value Gaps (FVG) / Imbalance:**
- 3-candle pattern where middle candle's body creates gap
- Price tends to return to fill FVGs (rebalance)
- Use as entry zones in trending markets
- Consequent Encroachment (CE): 50% of FVG = precision entry

**Kill Zones (ICT Time-Based):**
- London Open: 02:00-05:00 EST
- NY Open: 07:00-10:00 EST
- London Close: 10:00-12:00 EST
- Asian Session: 20:00-00:00 EST
- Major moves initiate during kill zones

**Premium/Discount:**
- Use Fibonacci 0-1 on swing
- Above 50% (0.5) = premium → look for sells
- Below 50% (0.5) = discount → look for buys
- Optimal Trade Entry (OTE): 62-79% retracement zone

### 2. Elliott Wave Theory

**Impulse Waves (5-wave structure):**
- Wave 1: Initial move, often weak
- Wave 2: Retracement (never beyond start of Wave 1), typically 50-61.8%
- Wave 3: Strongest, longest wave (never the shortest)
- Wave 4: Complex correction, doesn't enter Wave 1 territory
- Wave 5: Final push, often with divergence

**Corrective Waves (3-wave structure):**
- **Zigzag (5-3-5):** Sharp correction, A>B<C
- **Flat (3-3-5):** Sideways correction
- **Triangle (3-3-3-3-3):** Converging, A-B-C-D-E
- **Complex:** WXY, WXYXZ combinations

**Wave Rules (inviolable):**
1. Wave 2 never retraces more than 100% of Wave 1
2. Wave 3 is never the shortest impulse wave
3. Wave 4 never enters the price territory of Wave 1

**Wave Guidelines:**
- Wave 3 typically extends 161.8% of Wave 1
- Wave 5 target: 100% or 61.8% of Wave 1
- Alternation: if Wave 2 is sharp, Wave 4 is flat (and vice versa)
- Channel technique: draw channel between Waves 2-4 to project Wave 5

**Fibonacci Relationships:**
- Wave 2: 50%, 61.8%, 78.6% of Wave 1
- Wave 3: 161.8%, 200%, 261.8% of Wave 1
- Wave 4: 38.2%, 50% of Wave 3
- Wave 5: 100% of Wave 1, or 61.8% of Wave 1-3

### 3. Wyckoff Method

**Market Phases:**

**Accumulation (Bottom):**
1. PS (Preliminary Support) — first buying after extended decline
2. SC (Selling Climax) — high volume capitulation
3. AR (Automatic Rally) — bounce after SC
4. ST (Secondary Test) — retest SC area on lower volume
5. Spring — false break below SC, shakeout (key entry!)
6. SOS (Sign of Strength) — breakout above AR
7. LPS (Last Point of Support) — pullback to retest breakout

**Distribution (Top):**
1. PSY (Preliminary Supply) — first resistance after rally
2. BC (Buying Climax) — high volume exhaustion
3. AR (Automatic Reaction) — decline after BC
4. ST (Secondary Test) — retest BC area
5. UTAD (Upthrust After Distribution) — false breakout above BC
6. SOW (Sign of Weakness) — break below AR
7. LPSY (Last Point of Supply) — weak rally

**Wyckoff Laws:**
1. **Supply & Demand** — price moves based on imbalance
2. **Cause & Effect** — consolidation range = cause, move = effect (P&F count for targets)
3. **Effort vs Result** — volume (effort) should confirm price movement (result)

### 4. Harmonic Patterns

**Major Patterns (Fibonacci-based):**

- **Gartley (222):**
  - B = 61.8% of XA
  - C = 38.2-88.6% of AB
  - D = 78.6% of XA
  - PRZ (Potential Reversal Zone) at D

- **Butterfly:**
  - B = 78.6% of XA
  - D = 127-161.8% of XA
  - Extended pattern beyond X

- **Bat:**
  - B = 38.2-50% of XA
  - D = 88.6% of XA
  - Tightest PRZ → highest accuracy

- **Crab:**
  - B = 38.2-61.8% of XA
  - D = 161.8% of XA
  - Most extended pattern

- **Cypher:**
  - B = 38.2-61.8% of XA
  - C = 113-141.4% of XA
  - D = 78.6% of XC

**Trading Harmonics:**
- Enter at D point (PRZ) with tight stop
- Confirm with RSI divergence or candlestick pattern at PRZ
- Target 1: 38.2% of CD, Target 2: 61.8% of CD

### 5. Volume Profile & Market Profile

**Volume Profile:**
- **POC (Point of Control):** Price with highest volume — magnetic level
- **Value Area (VA):** 70% of volume — the "fair" range
- **VAH / VAL:** Value Area High / Low — act as S/R
- **HVN (High Volume Node):** Price levels of acceptance → support/resistance
- **LVN (Low Volume Node):** Price levels of rejection → price moves fast through these

**Market Profile (TPO):**
- **Initial Balance (IB):** First hour range — sets daily context
- **Single Prints:** Price visited only once → inefficiency, magnet for return
- **Profile Shapes:**
  - Normal/Gaussian: balanced, mean-reverting day
  - P-shape: short covering rally (distribution)
  - b-shape: long liquidation decline (accumulation)
  - D-shape: balanced trending day

**Auction Market Theory:**
- Market seeks fair value through auction process
- Imbalance: price moves directionally seeking opposing participants
- Balance: price rotates within value area
- Initiative vs Responsive activity

### 6. Order Flow Analysis

**Footprint Charts:**
- Delta: difference between buy and sell volume at each price
- Cumulative Delta: running total — divergence from price = key signal
- Absorption: large delta with no price movement → strong S/R
- Imbalance stacking: consecutive imbalances in one direction → strong momentum

**DOM (Depth of Market):**
- Iceberg orders: hidden size behind small visible orders
- Spoofing: large orders placed/cancelled to manipulate
- Stacking: multiple levels of large orders = institutional interest

## Multi-Timeframe Confluence

**The Power of 3 Timeframes:**
1. **Structure TF (Weekly/Daily):** Determine bias — bullish or bearish
2. **Setup TF (4H/1H):** Find specific pattern or setup
3. **Entry TF (15m/5m):** Precision entry with tight stop

**Confluence Checklist:**
- ✅ Higher TF trend direction
- ✅ Key level (OB, demand zone, harmonic PRZ, Wyckoff spring)
- ✅ Liquidity taken (sweep of highs/lows)
- ✅ FVG or imbalance present
- ✅ Time (kill zone or session open)
- ✅ Volume confirmation
- 4+ confluences = A+ setup
