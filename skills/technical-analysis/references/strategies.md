# Trading Strategies Reference

## Swing Trading (Days to Weeks)
- Use daily + 4H charts
- Enter on pullbacks to support in uptrend
- Indicators: RSI oversold + MACD bullish cross + volume
- Stop: below recent swing low
- Target: next resistance or Fibonacci extension

## Day Trading (Intraday)
- Use 15min + 5min charts, daily for context
- VWAP as key level — buy above, sell below
- Opening Range Breakout (first 15-30 min)
- Avoid trading first 5 min (volatility spike)
- Stop trading after 3 losses in a day

## Position Trading (Weeks to Months)
- Use weekly + daily charts
- Follow the 200 MA — be long above, cautious below
- Golden Cross / Death Cross for major signals
- Sector rotation analysis
- Earnings and macro events awareness

## Breakout Strategy
1. Identify consolidation (triangle, flag, range)
2. Wait for breakout with volume confirmation (>1.5x avg)
3. Enter on retest of broken level
4. Stop below breakout candle low
5. Target: measured move (pattern height projected)

## Mean Reversion
1. Wait for price to reach Bollinger Band extreme
2. Confirm with RSI overbought/oversold
3. Enter on reversal candle
4. Stop beyond the extreme
5. Target: middle band (SMA20)

## Divergence Trading
1. Price makes new high/low
2. Indicator (RSI/MACD) doesn't confirm
3. Wait for price to break recent structure
4. Enter with confirmation candle
5. Stop beyond the divergence extreme

## Multi-Timeframe Analysis
- **Higher TF (Weekly):** Determine trend direction
- **Middle TF (Daily):** Find trade setup
- **Lower TF (4H/1H):** Fine-tune entry
- Rule: Only trade in direction of higher timeframe trend

## Sector Analysis (Israeli Market)
- TA-35 index for overall market health
- Bank stocks: sensitive to interest rates
- Tech stocks: correlated with NASDAQ
- Real estate: watch Bank of Israel rate decisions
- Dual-listed stocks: check both TASE and US exchange

## Common Mistakes to Avoid
- Trading against the trend
- No stop-loss
- Overtrading (too many positions)
- Revenge trading after losses
- Moving stop-loss further away
- FOMO entries without setup
- Ignoring volume on breakouts
- Using too many indicators (analysis paralysis)
